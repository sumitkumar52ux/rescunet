    Offline Mesh Network

    When mobile networks and internet fail, small radios pass an SOS from one node to the next until it reaches a gateway that still has internet. This is a live simulation of that idea.

      📵 No internet? Use SMS

      Send an SMS to the RescuNet number. Write SOS, then medical or food if needed, then your latitude and longitude.

      SOS medical 25.61,85.16

      Settings

      Alert sound is enabled automatically after your first tap on this page. You can test it here (plays only on your device).

      🔒 The global siren can only be triggered by authorized disaster-management officials. Your SOS is private: it goes to nearby volunteers and the control room, never to the public.

      🔔 Background alerts

      Receive siren warnings, flood alerts and SOS notifications on this device even when the website is closed. Keep the phone sound on and Do-Not-Disturb off.

      iPhone/iPad: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen", open RescuNet from the home screen, and turn alerts on there.
